Spinner

Use a spinner to let users know content is being loaded.

Example

            <li>
                <mz-spinner></mz-spinner>
            </li>
            <li>
                <mz-spinner size="large"></mz-spinner>
            </li>
            <li>
                <mz-spinner appearance="secondary"></mz-spinner>
            </li>
            <li>
                <mz-spinner appearance="secondary" size="large"></mz-spinner>
            </li>
